Building an Exceptional, Inclusive, and
Award-Winning Culture

Our core values and success are directly linked to our presence-based culture where our daily operations and growth are powered by our ongoing commitment of presence for our clients, colleagues, and community.

Culture & Community

Our reputation as a leading Managed Services Provider (MSP) and IT Consultancy hinges on the quality of our people. We are all passionate about our individual set of skills that collectively come together to form a multi-dimensional presence. From our diverse workforce to our team-building programs, we strive to maintain an open and friendly culture for everyone.

PROVEN IMPACT AT SCALE

Why Work at SDI?

Benefits
We offer medical, dental, vision, life and disability insurance, commuter transit program, and more. And we also offer a 401(k) with a match, and profit-sharing. We even give you a phone allowance, and money to upgrade your phone.
Health and Wellness
We support and promote a healthy lifestyle inside and out – financially, physically, and emotionally. Our SDITOPIA program provides you with various wellness activities with incentives to create a more productive work environment.
Social Responsibility
Building on the SDI principle of corporate social responsibility, the SDI Team actively supports a number of diversity initiatives, internships, and philanthropies to make a noteworthy impact on our customers, employees, and community.

Culture is a critical aspect of any successful organization. Every day, the SDI Team’s presence is demonstrated  through:

Accountability
We believe that accountability is essential to our success,and we are committed to delivering high-quality work ontime and on budget.
Precision
Prioritizing precision ensures that we consistently deliver high-quality work that exceeds customer expectations.​
Innovation
We believe innovating is the key to succeeding, and we are committed to the exploration of new ideas and approaches to meet customer needs.
Ambition
We are passionate about what we do, and we are committed to achieving excellence. We are committed to pushing ourselves to new heights of achievement.
Connection
We believe that strong connections are the key to success, and we are committed to continually fostering a culture of teamwork and personal growth.​
Inclusion
We believe that diversity is essential to our success and we are committed to creating a workplace culture that celebrates and embraces our differences.​
